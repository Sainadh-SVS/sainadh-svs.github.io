About Japadhara

Japadhara is a thoughtful, privacy-focused devotional chant counter designed for spiritual seekers and mantra practitioners. Whether performing daily Japa, Navaratri sadhana, or personal meditation pledges, Japadhara helps you record, track, and sustain your spiritual discipline.

Core Highlights

📿 Mindful Japa Tracking

Track chant counts with quick presets (108, 1008), daily goal progress, and baseline adjustments.

🔒 Offline-First & Private

Your spiritual records stay on your device. Zero user profiling, zero advertisements, zero tracking.

☁️ Google Drive Backup

Optional cloud synchronization using strict per-file access (drive.file) to your own private Drive.

📊 Spreadsheet Export

Directly export your spiritual milestones into CSV spreadsheets viewable in Google Sheets or Excel.
